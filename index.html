3. What Debt Ledger does

Debt Ledger is a business record-keeping tool. Depending on your plan and the features enabled in your app version, it can help you keep debtor/customer records, record debts and payments, manage inventory, prepare receipts and PDF/Excel reports, set reminders, back up or synchronise selected information, and use OCR or AI to extract draft entries from documents.

Debt Ledger is not a bank, lender, credit bureau, payment processor, money transmitter, debt-collection agency, accounting firm or law firm. The app records information you enter. It does not lend money, collect a debtor's payment, move funds, verify that a debt is legally enforceable, or settle a payment between you and your customer. Recording “Cash”, “M-Pesa”, “bank” or another payment method is a bookkeeping entry; it does not itself send or receive money.

Reports, receipts and balances are generated from information in your ledger. They are tools to assist your record keeping and are not audited financial statements, tax advice, legal advice or proof that an underlying transaction occurred.

6. OCR, AI scanning and generated results

When you select local OCR, the app uses document-scanning and text-recognition components to extract text. When you select AI scanning, the image is resized/compressed and sent to the authenticated Debt Ledger backend, which forwards it to Google Cloud Vertex AI/Gemini for structured extraction.

AI results are suggestions, not verified facts. The system may omit, misread, misclassify or incorrectly associate a name, item, quantity, date or price. A missing or unreadable price may be returned as zero. You must review and edit every result before saving it, and you remain responsible for the final ledger entry and all financial calculations.

Do not use scan output as the sole basis for deciding whether a person owes money, whether a payment was made, or whether to take legal or financial action. We do not warrant that the service will recognise every image, handwriting style, language, currency or document layout. AI features may be unavailable, rate-limited or changed as models and providers change.
